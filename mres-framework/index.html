Driven by a Vision for Democratic Integrity

The Multi-Religion Election System (MRES) is an initiative developed by De Nya Svenskarna, a Sweden-based, globally oriented organization. We connect religion, democracy, and peacebuilding by proving that internal democratic reforms within faith communities can strengthen human rights, gender equality, and accountability.

Join a growing network of forward-thinking leaders who recognize that true secular democracy and lasting global peace require innovative, inclusive models like MRES.

"Regardless of whether the Multi-Religion Election holds official political significance today, our aim is to ensure that historical conflicts are not left for the next generation to resolve." — De Nya Svenskarna
