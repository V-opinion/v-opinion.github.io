Why This Initiative Matters

Across Europe, religious communities face a fundamental challenge: how to ensure equitable representation in democratic governance without compromising the secular nature of the state. Current systems often force a choice between marginalization or the entanglement of religion and partisan politics.

The Multi-Religion Election System (MRES) offers a proven, scalable solution. Inspired by the Nordic model of democratic church elections, MRES creates a dedicated, parallel avenue for religious representation—the Faith Representatives Chamber—ensuring all faith communities have an equitable voice while maintaining strict separation from secular politics.

This initiative seeks to bring the MRES framework to the European Parliament for formal consideration and adaptation across EU member states.

What This Achieves

⚖️

True Secular Governance

Ensures religious parties cannot participate in secular parliamentary elections, maintaining the fundamental separation of church and state.

🤝

Inclusive Representation

Guarantees equitable representation for all recognized faith communities, including minorities and marginalized groups.

🕊️

Long-Term Peacebuilding

Provides an institutional framework to resolve deep-rooted conflicts by addressing religious representation and power-sharing at their core.

📊

Democratic Integrity

Strengthens democratic institutions by creating clear, transparent channels for faith-based dialogue without partisan interference.
